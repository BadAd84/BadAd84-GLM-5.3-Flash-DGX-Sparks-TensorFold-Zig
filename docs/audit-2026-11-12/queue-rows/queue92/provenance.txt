  queue92  eleven stock boots, KNOBS=off, 21:57 to 04:18 UTC: s9a sm2 smz s13a ud1 ui1 smr s10a s9b s13b sm3
           (v1.2.9 twice, v1.2.10, v1.3.0 twice, the PDL commit, the item plan commit, main 1ece6e6db twice, main with PDL off, main with request starts)
  queue96  one boot, ut1: stock main at the tip 46c6ee229, KNOBS=off, 04:19 to 05:06 UTC (image built 04:27); score92.py reads it as its ut1 row
  queue97  (when it has run) tn1 and tn2: our stack re-seated on the tip

queue96 and queue97 are copies of queue92.sh run with ARMS set, so their logs carry the [q92] prefix and the QUEUE92-DONE marker; queue92.log and queue96.log sit beside the rows.
The main group of score92.py is mps (queue91's boot of stock main), sm2 and sm3; mps lives in queue-rows/queue91/f91-c.
